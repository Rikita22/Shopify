Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. As you use Spendly, we store the expense records you enter — including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you provide. We do not collect payment information or access your bank accounts.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate the Spendly service — to display your expense history, generate summaries, and support filtering and search. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond delivering the features of the app to you.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secured servers. We take reasonable measures to protect it from unauthorised access, including encrypted connections and access controls. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal data with third parties. We may use third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting services) to operate the application, but these providers are bound by confidentiality obligations and may only process your data on our behalf.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or would like to request deletion of your data, please reach out to us. You can submit a request through the app's profile settings or contact us directly at the email address listed on our support page.
